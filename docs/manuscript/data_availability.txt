The complete open-source project, including model architectures, multi-stage training pipelines, evaluation scripts, and multi-language localized documentation, is publicly available in our GitHub repository (https://github.com/GhadeerMostafa/EEG-MRANC) under the Creative Commons Attribution-ShareAlike 4.0 International (CC BY-SA 4.0) license. The pre-trained weights and clinical model checkpoints are proprietary assets and are not publicly hosted. To support academic validation and reproducibility, these weights will be shared on a case-by-case basis upon reasonable request for non-commercial research purposes by contacting the corresponding author.
