We presented MRANC, a Multi-Resolution Attention-Guided Neural Cleaner that decomposes scalp EEG into interpretable ocular, muscular, cardiac, and baseline artifact stems while enforcing an exact physics-locked reconstruction. Sequential Knowledge-Stacking transfers representation learning from controlled benchmarks through DEAP and SEED into CHB-MIT clinical windows, and a zero-initialized multi-scale attention adapter delivers event-aligned saliency without destroying stacked features at initialization.

MRANC advances EEG preprocessing from opaque noise suppression toward accountable, stem-resolved signal explanation. We anticipate impact on methodological research (structured artifact modeling) and translational pipelines (ICU monitoring, ambulatory epilepsy care, and multi-center tele-EEG), where clinicians must see not only cleaner traces, but also what was removed, when, and why.
